# Made to Fight, Made to Represent

A digital film-festival experience that connects a visitor's taste in screen combat to the cultural themes of five Asian films. Terms below describe the supplied `web.pdf` brief.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A person exploring the festival through their film-viewing preferences.
_Avoid_: Player, fighter, customer

**Featured film**:
One of the five films selected by the brief: Hero (2002), Ip Man (2008), The Rebel (2007), The Protector (2005), or 13 Assassins (2010).
_Avoid_: Product, course

**Combat style**:
The brief's thematic label connecting a featured film with its portrayal of combat. These labels describe the festival's framing and are not intended as a uniform martial-arts classification.
_Avoid_: Skill level, fighting ability

**Quiz**:
The three questions about a visitor's preferred fighter qualities, fight-scene qualities, and cultural interests.
_Avoid_: Assessment, diagnosis, training test

**Film match**:
The featured film recommended by the visitor's three answers; it reflects viewing preferences rather than actual fighting ability.
_Avoid_: Personality diagnosis, proficiency score

**Cultural exploration**:
The interpretive content connecting screen combat with the identity, heritage, and themes presented in a featured film.
_Avoid_: Training, combat tutorial

**Screening**:
A scheduled festival presentation of a featured film. The supplied brief does not establish any actual screening dates, times, venues, or booking arrangements.
_Avoid_: Trailer, preview

**Film preview**:
A short preview of a featured film, distinct from a complete film or festival screening.
_Avoid_: Watch full film, screening
